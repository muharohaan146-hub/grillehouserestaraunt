Fire-kissed perfection.
South African soul.

Grille House is a premium steakhouse rooted in the art of charcoal grilling and the warmth of South African and Portuguese culinary tradition. From slow-smoked Brisket and Short Ribs to flame-grilled Piri Piri chicken, every dish is crafted with obsessive attention to heat, time, and flavour.

Slow-Smoked Meats

Premium Brisket, Short Ribs & Boerewors smoked low and slow over real charcoal.

Premium Steaks

Ribeye, Rump, Tenderloin & T-Bone — graded, aged, and cooked to your exact liking.

SA & Portuguese Fusion

Authentic flavours from two great grilling traditions — Pap, Malva Pudding, and Piri Piri.
